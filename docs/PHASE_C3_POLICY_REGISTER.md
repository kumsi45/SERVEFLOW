## Retention research and proposed schedule — not approved

ServeFlow's intended launch jurisdictions are Ethiopia and Kenya. Ethiopia's [Personal Data Protection Proclamation No. 1321/2024](https://justice.gov.et/en/law/personal-data-protection-proclamation/) is listed as in force by its Ministry of Justice. Kenya's [Data Protection Act, section 39](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf) and [General Regulations, regulation 19](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf) require purpose-based retention and a schedule with periodic review. These sources do **not** establish the category periods proposed below. Tax, accounting, consumer, employment, payment-provider and cross-border obligations still need local legal review in both countries.

The periods below are planning proposals only. Start dates, tenant/controller responsibility, backup copies, exceptions and deletion method require approval. No automated deletion is authorized.

| Category | Proposed review/retention period | Reason and legal verification | Access and end-of-period proposal |
|---|---|---|---|
| Financial records, payment evidence, reconciliation | **7 years** after the relevant financial year closes | Candidate audit/dispute window; verify Ethiopia and Kenya tax/accounting duties and payment-provider terms. | Owner historical view; restricted finance/admin evidence; review legal hold, then secure deletion/anonymization only if approved. |
| Orders, receipts and operational records | **7 years** where linked to financial records; **2 years** for nonfinancial operational telemetry | Preserve invoice context; verify hospitality/customer record obligations. | Tenant-scoped historical view; minimize customer identifiers when no longer necessary. |
| Customer information and feedback | **2 years** after last relevant interaction, unless tied to a retained dispute/financial record | Purpose-limited service and complaint handling; verify privacy and consumer obligations. | Authorized tenant staff only; remove or anonymize identifiers after review. |
| Administrator decisions and security/audit events | **7 years** for entitlement/payment decisions; **3 years** for routine security events | Explain access decisions and investigate abuse; verify legal and security needs. | Security/admin reviewers; protect integrity; review holds before disposal. |
| Uploaded payment receipts and other files | **7 years** for evidence attached to financial decisions; **1 year** after replacement/closure for ordinary temporary assets | Align evidence with record; minimize unnecessary copies; verify provider/storage terms. | Signed, scoped access; remove file and derivatives after approved review. |
| Rejected applications | **2 years** after final decision | Support reapplication history and disputes while limiting applicant data; verify privacy obligations. | Platform reviewers only; anonymize or delete on approved schedule. |
| Expired invitation token digests and delivery metadata | **90 days** after expiry for token digest; **2 years** for nonsecret audit metadata | Short replay-investigation window; preserve decisions without recoverable token. | Security/admin only; token digest removal separately reviewed. |
| Onboarding and readiness evidence | **2 years** after contract end; longer if attached to a dispute/decision | Explain activation and setup; verify contractual obligations. | Owner view of own status; admin review; minimize personal details. |
| Backups and disaster recovery copies | **35-day rolling** candidate, subject to provider capability and legal holds | Bound recovery exposure; verify restore objectives, immutable backups and regional storage. | Recovery operators only; expiry from backups must follow approved runbook. |

Legal holds, disputes, fraud investigations and applicable statutory duties may override a proposed period. The final schedule must say who approves an exception, how copies are located, when review occurs, and how deletion or anonymization is verified. Historical owner access and retention duties must be reconciled before any destructive automation.
