# ServeFlow Food Photography Style Guide v1

Status: Frozen visual contract  
Scope: Every ServeFlow Smart Menu master image  
Generation: Not authorized in this phase

## Global Photography Identity

Every image must look as though it was produced by one professional restaurant photographer for one premium global collection. The identity is professional restaurant food photography with a natural, modern, premium, minimal, authentic luxury-cafe character.

The food remains culturally and visually correct. The photographic treatment remains consistent across Ethiopian dishes, Western dishes, bakery products, coffee, juice, and bar beverages.

## Photography Rules

- Show one authentic dish or beverage only.
- Use realistic ingredients, texture, cooking level, color, and portion size.
- Present restaurant-serving quality without artificial perfection or decoration.
- Keep the entire plate, bowl, cup, or glass visible.
- Do not crop the food or duplicate the serving.
- Do not include branding, text, watermarks, people, or restaurant interiors.

## Lighting Rules

- Use soft daylight with balanced exposure.
- Preserve natural highlights and gentle realistic shadows.
- Maintain consistent, warm but realistic white balance.
- Never use dramatic, colored, HDR-exaggerated, or glowing light.

## Composition Rules

- One dish only, centered with clean spacing.
- Entire serving vessel visible and food clearly dominant.
- No multiple meals, duplicate servings, extra plates, clutter, or floating ingredients.
- No forks, knives, phones, hands, or people.

## Camera Rules

- Top-down: pizza, salads, breakfast platters, and other intentionally arranged flat dishes.
- 45°: burgers, coffee, cake, soup, pasta, plated Ethiopian dishes, and most restaurant meals.
- Front: bread, bakery products, and drinks when the vessel profile is important.
- Dish specifications may select only Top-down, 45°, or Front.

## Plate and Vessel Rules

- Default to modern premium white ceramic without logos or patterns.
- Use clean neutral drinkware appropriate to the beverage.
- Colored or traditional serving vessels are allowed only when culturally required.
- Ethiopian dishes may use correct injera presentation and authentic serving bowls.
- Coffee may use a traditional cup where appropriate.

## Background Rules

- Primary background: clean white marble.
- Alternative background: light gray matte.
- Never use a dark background, wood table, patterned tablecloth, messy table, kitchen scene, people, or restaurant interior.

## Color Rules

- Natural, warm, realistic color.
- Balanced and consistent white balance across the collection.
- No oversaturation, fake contrast, artificial colors, or category-specific color grading.

## Garnish Rules

- Use only garnish authentic to the named dish.
- Kitfo may include authentic ayib and gomen.
- Tibs may include authentic onion, jalapeño, or lemon.
- Coffee may show natural foam; cappuccino may show restrained authentic foam art.
- Burgers may show their authentic lettuce, tomato, or pickle components.
- Never add random herbs, flowers, or unrealistic decoration.

## Category Exceptions

- Ethiopian dishes: correct injera, traditional plating, authentic bowls, ingredients, and accompaniments.
- Coffee: appropriate modern or traditional cup and natural foam where applicable.
- Western foods: modern premium restaurant presentation on white ceramic.
- Bakery: clean bakery-display presentation while preserving the global background and lighting.
- Bar: professional beverage glassware, realistic liquid color, no people or venue background.

Exceptions preserve authenticity; they never override the lighting, cleanliness, realism, background, branding, or negative rules.

## Image Quality and Output Contract

- Master resolution: 2048 × 2048 pixels.
- Master aspect ratio: 1:1.
- Required later derivatives: 1280, 1024, 768, 512, and 320 pixels.
- Delivery format: WebP.
- Ultra-realistic, high-detail, sharp focus, clean edges, natural color, balanced exposure.

## Global Negative Prompt

No text, no watermark, no logo, no human hands, no people, no forks, no knives, no phones, no extra plates, no messy table, no dark background, no wood table, no patterned tablecloth, no restaurant interior, no kitchen background, no artificial colors, no oversaturation, no fake contrast, no HDR exaggeration, no artificial glow, no AI artifacts, no floating ingredients, no duplicate food, no multiple meals, no cropped food, no random herbs, no flowers, no unrealistic decoration, no cartoon appearance, no painting style, no illustration.

- [ ] Modern white ceramic or justified cultural vessel
- [ ] Natural, consistent color and white balance
- [ ] Sharp, realistic, professional restaurant quality
- [ ] No prohibited content or visual artifacts

- [ ] Matches the ServeFlow global visual family

This v1 guide is the immutable visual baseline. Future providers may change, but this identity and its rules do not.
